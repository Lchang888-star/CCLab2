What we know so far about [ Felis Spacius ]? Felis Spacius heavily resembles a normal house cat on Earth with similar behavioral tendencies. So far only one has been found in all of space. It is a very simple creature that constantly moves around in space flying from solar system to solar system. Our research shows that their may be a huge population of them that we have not discovered yet. Their ability to morphe into different shapes allows them to hideout and not be found. A bite from one also contains a deadly venom equivalent of a black mamba.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Approach this creature with caution. If disturbed suddenly this creature will attack with blood red eyes. Keep your distance and it will continue to float around while in your presence.
